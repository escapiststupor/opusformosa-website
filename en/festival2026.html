Opus Formosa's inaugural annual festival spans three cities across Taiwan, bringing together world-class artists for five performances in two series. From the National Concert Hall in Taipei to the Weiwuying Concert Hall in Kaohsiung and the National Taichung Theater, each concert takes place in one of Taiwan's most celebrated concert halls.
