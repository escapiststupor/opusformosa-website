Friends of Opus Formosa

To thank the friends who help us share the music we love, individuals, organizations, and companies who support the 2026 Opus Music Festival directly through Opus Formosa with a single ticket/donation package of NT$50,000 or more will be acknowledged in the official program as a “Friend of the 2026 Opus Music Festival.”

Benefits

  1. Flexible combinations: Combine Opus Formosa VIP reserved seats across concerts. The total simply needs to meet the qualification amount.
  2. Exclusive reserved seats: Purchase centrally located reserved seats that are not released for public sale on OPENTIX. Final seat arrangements depend on remaining availability and team confirmation.
  3. Donation receipt: This package will be handled as a donation to an arts and culture activity with complimentary guest tickets. A donation receipt will be issued according to applicable rules.

How the donation receipt may be used: For Taiwan tax filing, the donation receipt may be used as supporting documentation for charitable donation deductions. Individuals may include it under itemized deductions, generally capped at 20% of consolidated gross income; companies may book it as an expense or loss, generally capped at 10% of taxable income. Actual deductibility depends on filing status, standard vs. itemized deductions, and review by the tax authority.

Notes:

Limited seats: VIP reserved seats are limited and available while they last. Program acknowledgment: Payment and required information must be completed before the program printing deadline; late submissions cannot be included in this year’s printed program. No additional discount: This package cannot be combined with OPENTIX discounts. Guest ticket handling: Tickets are complimentary guest tickets issued as part of a donation package, not regular tickets. Please confirm first: To have the amount counted toward Friends qualification and donation receipt issuance, please confirm seats with the team before payment. Please do not check out directly on OPENTIX.

Inquiry And Confirmation Process

  1. Review the seat map snapshots and prepare your preferred concerts, quantities, and seat preferences.
  2. Contact Opus Formosa by Email or LINE (@opusformosa) so the team can confirm seat availability and the final amount.
  3. After the team confirms seat availability and the final amount, complete the bank transfer and reply with the information needed for payment confirmation and donation receipt issuance.
