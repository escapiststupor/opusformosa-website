Thank you to all who support our mission to promote classical music arts in Taiwan

Opus Formosa gratefully acknowledges its generous supporters, whose commitment amplifies the vital impact of education, arts, and culture in Taiwan. Philanthropic support powers innovative programming on Opus Formosa stages, provides scholarships for emerging musicians, expands year-round free and affordable concerts, and brings arts education and enrichment to public schools, libraries, and senior centers.
